Prime Numbers

What are Prime Numbers?

A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 that can only be divided evenly by 1 and itself. This means you can't break it into equal smaller whole number groups without leaving a remainder.

Important Rules for Prime Numbers:

- Only two factors: A prime number has precisely two distinct positive divisors: 1 and the number itself.

- Must be greater than 1: Because 1 only has 1 factor: 1. And itself is 1. That's why prime numbers must be greater than 1.
